I Day One

Arrival & the First Aarti

The city meets you slowly. A private transfer, a handwritten note waiting at check-in, no itinerary pressing on the afternoon — and then, as evening falls, the ghats begin to glow.

Afternoon

Welcome to Kashi

  • Private AC vehicle pickup from airport or railway station, with a greeting from our professional driver and packed bottle water
  • Check-in at a premium property or boutique hotel; welcome kit — drink, handwritten city note, printed itinerary booklet
  • Briefing with your Kashi companion, then a quick rest — no rush, no itinerary, optional light room service or a short nap
Evening

The Ganga Aarti

  • Ghat transfer with your local companion, premium seating at Dashashwamedh Ghat for the main Ganga Aarti ceremony
  • Diya floating and kulhad chai on the river as the crowds disperse
Night

Street Food Walk — Chowk

  • The illuminated lanes of the old city: tamatar chaat, aloo tikki, Banarasi paan, gol-gappe, thandai, palangtod mithai (seasonal)
  • Every dish with its full story — the vendor, the history, the craft behind it — through hidden bylanes and lit temples
II Day Two — The Main Event

The Illumination

Scroll to watch the day turn to night the way it really happens on the ghats — from a calm fort morning to 84 ghats lit in one unbroken line of fire.

11:00 AM — Ramnagar Fort

The Festival Begins

Entry through Akashganga's dedicated QR channel — no queue, fort grounds still calm. A guided museum walk through royal palanquins, vintage cars and silk regalia, then a live Banarasi weaving & zardozi workshop, closing with a proper seated Banaras Hi-Tea.

2:30 – 3:45 PM

Music, Lanterns & Fire

Benaras Gharana classical music into Kathak, timed against the fort and the Ganga below as light turns to gold. Then sky lanterns released from the fort precinct as firecrackers arc above the river — the formal close of the land festival.

4:00 – Dusk

Aboard the Cruise

A private, QR-managed ghat transfer — none of the chaos of the public boats — onto a boat positioned facing the full length of Varanasi's western bank. On-seat refreshments, and the first diyas being lit from waterline to highest step.

Dusk – 8:30 PM

84 Ghats, One Line of Fire

All 84 ghats lit from waterline to rooftop, fireworks arcing overhead, your storyteller narrating each one — Dashashwamedh, Manikarnika, Kedar, Harishchandra, Assi. Then ashore to a Ramnagar Fort dinner: buffet on Premium, private chef-attended fine dining on Luxury.

Morning — Free & Unscheduled

The City Prepares

  • Sleep in, or walk to the nearest ghat on your own — sit with a chai and watch Varanasi prepare for the night ahead
  • Guide available for an optional independent ghat walk, 8:00–10:00 AM; nothing scheduled — be ready to leave the hotel by 10:30 AM
10:30 AM

Departure for Ramnagar Fort

  • The single most important crowd decision of the day — crossing before 11:00 AM keeps the journey to 20–30 min; post-2:00 PM it can take 60–90 min
11:00 AM – 12:15 PM

Ramnagar Fort Museum Walk

  • Royal palanquins, vintage cars, antique weapons, astronomical instruments, silk regalia of the Maharajas of Kashi — quiet and unhurried, first in ahead of the afternoon crowd
12:15 – 1:30 PM

Handicraft Workshop & Cultural Exhibition

  • Live Banarasi weaving demonstration and zardozi embroidery workshop, with guided artisan interaction
1:30 – 2:30 PM

Banaras Hi-Tea

  • A proper seated meal — tamatar chaat, litti chokha, baati, thandai, seasonal mithai
2:30 – 3:45 PM

Classical Music & Kathak Performance

  • Benaras Gharana classical music followed by Kathak, against the fort and the Ganga below, timed to golden hour
3:45 PM

Lantern & Fireworks Celebration

  • Sky lanterns released from the fort precinct as firecrackers arc above the Ganga — the formal close of the land festival
4:00 – 4:30 PM

Private Ghat Transfer & Cruise Boarding

  • QR-managed, calm private boarding — no public-ghat chaos — with on-seat refreshments served immediately
4:30 PM – Dusk

The Golden Hour on the River

  • All 84 ghats in a single unbroken view; diyas lit from waterline to highest step, temple bells and conch shells carrying across the river
Dusk – 8:30 PM

Full Dev Deepawali Illumination

  • An unbroken line of fire across the horizon; your storyteller narrates each ghat — Dashashwamedh, Manikarnika, Kedar, Harishchandra, Assi — its name, deity, legend and history
8:30 PM

Disembark & Move to Dinner

  • The boat returns to the Ramnagar private ghat — a short walk to the dinner venue within the fort precinct
Post-Cruise Dinner

Two Experiences — Premium & Luxury

  • Premium — Ramnagar Fort Buffet Dinner: dal baati, lal maas, Banarasi korma, assorted breads, mithai counter, thandai
  • Luxury — Ramnagar Fort Special Fine Dining: a private, chef-attended multi-course dinner — shorba, dum preparations, slow-cooked meats, fine breads, a seasonal dessert course
~10:30 – 11:00 PM

Return Transfer to Hotel

  • Private vehicle waiting at Ramnagar. Rest — Day 2 is complete.
III Day Three

Blessings & Departure

Kashi says goodbye slowly too — a dawn walk, a last kachori and chai, a marble temple at the edge of the old city, and a note waiting for you at checkout.

Early Morning

Final Ghat Walk

  • Assi Ghat northward in the early light — Subah-e-Banaras, yoga practitioners, Vedic chanting at the river
  • North through Tulsi Ghat and past Chet Singh Ghat, an ancient Akhada, and a farewell chai at a heritage ghat
Breakfast

Farewell Street Food — Assi & Lanka

  • Kachori sabzi, jalebi, kulhad chai, lassi — the definitive Banarasi morning spread
  • Your companion shares final stories and reflections about the city over breakfast
Late Morning

BHU & New Vishwanath Temple

  • Banaras Hindu University's grand central avenue — colonial Indo-Saracenic architecture, peacocks on the lawns
  • The New Vishwanath Temple — 77m of white Makrana marble, unhurried darshan away from the old-city crowds
Checkout

Farewell, Kashi

  • Return to the property for packing, then a private AC vehicle transfer to the airport or railway station
  • A handwritten note from your Kashi companion, and a surprise gift

Booking Policies

Good to Know Before You Book

Dev Deepawali is a fixed, festival-dated experience with limited third-party allocations. Please read the policies below carefully before confirming.

A confirmed booking requires a 50% advance payment at the time of reservation. The remaining 50% balance must be paid no later than 15 days prior to the travel date, or as otherwise communicated at the time of booking. Bookings are confirmed only after the advance payment is realised, and remain subject to availability of Akashganga festival seats, cruise capacity, and hotel rooms. Given the highly limited nature of these allocations, early booking is strongly recommended. If the balance is not received by the due date, Wandermate reserves the right to cancel the booking, and the advance paid shall stand forfeited per the cancellation policy below.

Fully inclusive of GST and all applicable government taxes, with no hidden charges beyond what is explicitly listed under Exclusions. Prices are subject to change for new bookings made closer to the event date, depending on Akashganga and hotel availability.

This is a strict no-refund booking, owing to the fixed, festival-dated nature of the package and the limited third-party allocations it depends on — Akashganga festival seats, cruise capacity, and hotel block bookings. Once the 50% advance is paid, no cancellation, refund, or partial refund will be processed under any circumstances, including a change in travel plans, illness, visa issues, or personal emergencies. If the balance 50% has also been paid, the full amount remains non-refundable. Wandermate strongly recommends guests purchase independent travel insurance to cover any unforeseen circumstances.

Because Dev Deepawali is a fixed annual date tied to the lunar calendar, rescheduling to an alternate date is not possible within the same booking. Name changes or guest substitutions may be considered case-by-case, subject to approval, and must be requested at least 7 days prior to the event date.

Wandermate is not liable for cancellations, delays, or itinerary changes caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control — natural calamities, government restrictions, political disturbances, extreme weather, or partner/vendor cancellations. Wandermate will make reasonable efforts to offer an alternative experience of comparable value, though no cash refund will be issued. Timings, sequence of activities, and specific inclusions may be modified by Wandermate or its partners — Akashganga and the partner hotels — for operational, safety, weather, or crowd-related reasons; any change will be communicated at the earliest opportunity, with the overall experience and value kept consistent with what was originally promised.

Guests are expected to follow the timing and instructions provided by Wandermate guides and Akashganga staff, particularly around boarding times for the cruise and entry to the festival — both are time-critical and cannot be relaxed once missed. Wandermate is not responsible for guests missing scheduled activities due to late arrival at designated meeting points. Guests with medical conditions are advised to inform Wandermate at the time of booking so appropriate arrangements can be made in advance. Valid government-issued photo identification is mandatory for hotel check-in and may be required for entry to the Akashganga festival and cruise.

Wandermate acts as a facilitator, coordinating accommodation, transport, guide services, and third-party experiences including the Akashganga festival and cruise.
